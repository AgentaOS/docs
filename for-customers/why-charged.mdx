## A charge from AgentaOS

This charge is for a digital product or subscription you bought. AgentaOS is the merchant of record that handled the payment on the seller's behalf, so your statement shows `AGENTAOS*` followed by the seller's name, for example `AGENTAOS* KNEEGUARD`. This is normal and expected.

## Find your purchase

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    Search your inbox for your AgentaOS receipt. It lists the product name, the seller, and the amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match the amount and date">
    Compare the amount and date on the receipt to the charge on your statement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you use more than one email address, try the others: you may have bought with a different one.

## I still do not recognize the charge

[Contact us](https://agentaos.ai) with the exact amount, the date it appeared, and the last 4 digits of the card or account charged. We will identify the product and seller for you.

## I want to cancel a subscription

Contact the seller to cancel, or cancel in the seller's own software or app. Your receipt names the seller: the email is titled "Receipt from" and the seller's name. To reach the seller, reply to the receipt email: your reply goes to the seller. Canceling stops future renewals, and you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

## I want a refund

Refund policies are set by each seller. Contact the seller first, using the support email on their website or your receipt. If the seller does not respond within 7 days, [contact us](https://agentaos.ai) and we will help.

## I think this charge is fraudulent

If you believe your payment details were used without your permission, [contact us immediately](https://agentaos.ai) with the amount, the date, and the last 4 digits of the card.

## The AgentaOS name on your statement

AgentaOS is the merchant of record, the legal seller on the transaction, so your statement shows `AGENTAOS*` followed by the seller's name, for example `AGENTAOS* KNEEGUARD`. See [How merchant of record works](/mor/how-it-works).
