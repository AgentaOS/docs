As a platform you can take a **share** of every sale a client business makes. You set it per business. Another app of your own company carries no share.

## How the share is worked out

The share is a **percentage of the sale's price before VAT, plus a fixed amount** per sale.

On a €100 sale (before VAT) with a 10% + €0.50 share, you get €10.50.

- **VAT is left out.** We are the Merchant of Record and remit the VAT; it is never part of your share.
- **Our own fee stays on the total paid**, as for any sale. See [Pricing](/mor/pricing). The client keeps what remains after our fee and your share.
- **The share is fixed on each sale when it is made.** Changing it later affects new sales only. Past sales keep the share they were made with.

## Set the share

The share is set in the dashboard only. There is no API for it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your businesses">
    **Settings → Business → Businesses you manage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set share">
    Click **Set share** on the business, enter the percentage and the fixed amount. The editor shows what the client keeps on an example sale, after our fee and yours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can read the current share on each business as `platformFee` (`{ bps, fixedMinor }`: basis points, where `1000` is 10%, and the fixed part in minor units). See [Business fields](/sdk/pay-businesses#business-fields).

## When you are paid

Your share is paid to you with **your own payout**, once that sale's reserve period has cleared, the same wait as the client's money from that sale.

The client must be in good standing at that point: verified, and not on hold. A share from a client that is not yet verified waits until they are.

## See what is coming

The **Platform fees** card on the **Payments** page shows each day's fees with its date: the next five days first, then one total for later. A day where the client is not yet in good standing says it is paid once that client is verified.

For a quick total per business, `feesThisMonth` on each [business](/sdk/pay-businesses#business-fields) gives your share of its live sales this month, per currency, ready to show.
