A **platform** is an AgentaOS account that manages other businesses. You build software for other people: an agency that launches products for clients, a site builder, a marketplace of apps. Each of your clients gets its own **business** on AgentaOS. It sells under its own name, gets its own invoices and payouts, and you run it with your own API key.

AgentaOS stays the Merchant of Record for every sale, yours and your clients'.

## Two kinds of business

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="A client's business" icon="building">
    Someone else's company. It is verified on its own, including an identity check that only the client can complete. You can take a share of each of its sales.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Another app of your own company" icon="layer-group">
    A second product you own ("same company"). It is verified and priced with you. It carries no share and no identity check of its own.
  </Card>
</Columns>

You choose when you create the business: `sameLegalEntity: true` in the SDK, `--same-company` in the CLI.

## Who does what

| Step | Who does it |
|---|---|
| Create the business | You |
| Invite the client to log in (optional) | You. We email the invitation, or you send the link yourself. |
| File the business details, product and declarations | You or the client. If you file it, the review records that the platform filed it, and our reviewers see that. |
| Identity check | **Only the client.** You can generate the link and send it; you cannot complete it for them. |
| Create products, checkouts and payment links | You, acting as the business, or the client |
| Set your share of its sales | You, in the dashboard only |
| Receive its webhook events | Your own webhook endpoint, automatically |

Most clients never log in. You can run everything for them and only send them the identity check link.

Removing a business you manage, or transferring it to another platform, is not in the dashboard today. Contact us at contact@agentaos.ai.

<Note>
White-label: create the business with `sendInvitationEmail: false` and we send no email. You get `inviteUrl` back and send it to your client yourself, under your own brand.
</Note>

## Acting as a business

Every API call can run as a business you manage. Send its id in the `AgentaOS-Account` header, or pass `{ business }` in the SDK, or `--business <id>` in the CLI. Without it, the call acts on your own account.

```typescript
const agentaos = new AgentaOS(process.env.AGENTAOS_API_KEY!);

// One call only
await agentaos.checkouts.create({ amount: 49, currency: 'EUR' }, { business: biz.id });

// Or a client on which every call runs as the business
const client = new AgentaOS(process.env.AGENTAOS_API_KEY!, { business: biz.id });
```

Only your own key or session may act for a business you manage. Any other id returns `403 You cannot act as this business.` See [Acting for a business](/sdk/pay-businesses#acting-for-a-business).
