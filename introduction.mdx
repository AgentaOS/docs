## AgentaOS as your merchant of record

AgentaOS is a billing and payments platform for AI founders, and your **merchant of record**: the legal seller of your product. Your buyer pays AgentaOS, and AgentaOS takes the payment, charges the VAT, issues the invoice and pays you out. As the registered seller, AgentaOS carries the payment and tax liability, so you do not register for tax abroad, file a cross-border return, or reconcile three processors. AgentaOS is PCI compliant. See [How it works](/mor/how-it-works).

## Quick start

Sign up at [app.agentaos.ai](https://app.agentaos.ai). No card is required, and you start in test mode with test money.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a product">
    Open **Products** and click **New product**. Give it a name and a price, choose one-time or subscription, and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link">
    Click **Copy link** and send it anywhere: an email, a chat message, or a QR code. One link can be paid many times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the test payment land">
    Open the link and pay with the test card `4242 4242 4242 4242`, any future expiry, and any CVC. The payment shows on **Home** and in **Transactions**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To integrate with code, follow the [code integration](/getting-started/quickstart#code-integration) steps in the quickstart.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How fast can I start accepting payments?">
    Minutes in test mode. Create an account, grab a test key, and create your first checkout. Going live needs a short business verification: see [Account review](/mor/account-review).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What payment methods do you support?">
    Card, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Cards work in 135+ currencies, from buyers worldwide.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which countries and taxes do you cover?">
    AgentaOS charges EU consumers VAT at the Estonian rate while its EU sales are under the €10,000 threshold, and charges no tax to buyers outside the EU. See [How tax works](/mor/tax#the-rate-on-eu-sales-today) and [Supported countries](/mor/supported-countries).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can my AI agents manage my store?">
    Yes. Point any AI agent at `SKILL.md`, or connect the MCP server, and it can create products, read customers and subscriptions, and send receipts on your behalf.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I bought something and see AgentaOS on my statement">
    AgentaOS is the merchant of record, so AgentaOS appears as the seller. See [Why was I charged](/for-customers/why-charged).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
