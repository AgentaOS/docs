The Revenue & Pricing Audit is your free AI pricing review. In the words of the **Go live** page, our pricing agent reads your pricing page and tells you what your pricing model is costing you, where you lose revenue after the sale, and what refunds and chargebacks take back.

The audit has a list price of €299. It is included with your AgentaOS account: the dashboard shows the price struck out, next to the label **Included**. It usually arrives within 24 hours, as a PDF, in the app and in your inbox.

In the dashboard the audit is yours once you verify your business: the **Go live** page shows it sealed until then, and it unlocks the moment your ID check is handed in. If you asked for it on the agentaos.ai home page, it is already yours and nothing is held back.

## A growth merchant of record

A merchant of record sells your product in its own name, and AgentaOS is the merchant of record on every sale. See [How merchant of record works](/mor/how-it-works). The agentaos.ai home page defines the growth merchant of record:

> A Merchant of Record sells your product in its own name: it takes the payment, files the tax and handles disputes. A growth Merchant of Record also helps you earn more. AgentaOS includes an AI pricing agent that finds why users don't pay and tells you what to charge.

The home page FAQ adds: "Our AI pricing agent looks at your product and pricing page, scores seven areas, and sends you a score out of 100 with the fixes worth the most." The Revenue & Pricing Audit is that report. Your published audit carries a score from 0 to 100 and a grade.

### From Home

Until your first live sale, **Home** carries one card with your next step. While the audit is still sealed for you, the line under the step says what verifying unlocks: real payments, payouts to your bank, and your free Revenue & Pricing Audit. From the moment it is unlocked, the line is the link **Get your Revenue & Pricing Audit**, which opens the same form as on the **Go live** page; once asked for, it shows where the audit stands.

## Information we ask for

We need a live web page where we can see your price. The form asks four questions. Only the first one is required.

| Screen | Question | Your answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Where can we see it?** | The full address of your product page, starting with `https://` |
| 2 | **What does it do?** | One sentence. Optional. |
| 3 | **What kind of business is it?** | **SaaS / software**, **Digital goods or content**, **Professional services**, **A marketplace**, **Physical goods**, or **Something else**. Optional. |
| 4 | **How do buyers get it?** | **Instantly, online**, **As a service, over time**, **Shipped to them**, or **Something else**. Optional. |

The form says the last question is "The biggest single driver of dispute risk, so it shapes the report." The home-page path sends your link only.

## The finished audit

The audit is a PDF. When it is ready, three things change:

- You get an email with the subject "Welcome. Your pricing audit is in." It lists what the report covers, and its **Read your audit** button opens the **Go live** page.
- The report on **Go live** changes to **Your audit is ready**, with your grade on its cover and the score beside it, and the button **Read your audit**, which opens the PDF.
- **Home** shows **Your audit is ready** and **Read it**, until your first live sale.

- How your pricing compares to the rest of your category.

## Use the findings

To act on a recommendation, change your products and plans:

- **A new price or a new plan.** Each price is its own product. A monthly plan and a yearly plan are two products. See [Products and payment links](/payments/payment-links).
- **A trial, or an upgrade path.** Set the trial on the plan, and move subscribers between plans. See [Subscriptions](/payments/subscriptions).
- **A launch offer.** Give buyers a code to type at checkout. See [Discount codes](/payments/discount-codes).

To ask a question about your audit, reply to the email it came with.

## One audit per business

Each business gets one audit. When you send the form again, we keep your first request and update the link and answers that you gave. After the audit is published, the report on **Go live** shows **Read your audit** in place of **Get my audit**.
