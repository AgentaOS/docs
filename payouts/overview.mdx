Your card and bank sales collect in one AgentaOS balance per currency, EUR or USD. Payouts run twice a month, on the 1st and the 15th, and send the available part of that balance to your payout account: a bank account, or a stablecoin wallet if AgentaOS turned that on for you. Each payout comes with a payout statement and a self-billed invoice for your books.

Before your first payout, you need three things:

- An approved business review. See [Verify your business](/mor/verification) and [Go live](/guides/go-live).
- A bank account in the currency of your balance. See [Add a bank account](/payouts/payout-accounts).
- The identity check, done once by an owner or admin in live mode. It starts from **Home** or **Balances** when you have money to pay out. See [The identity check](/mor/verification#the-identity-check).

## Your balance

In the dashboard, open **Balances**. The **Overview** tab shows your **Balance** for each currency, split into rows that add up to the **Total**:

| Row | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Available now** | Cleared money. The next payout sends it. |
| **On the way** | Sales that are still clearing, with the date each amount becomes available. |
| **Reserve** | "Safety hold on new accounts". The row shows the date the reserve frees up. |
| **To your bank** | A payout in progress. It leaves your balance when it lands. |
| **Paused (disputed)** | Money from a sale that a buyer disputes, held until the dispute ends. |

A new sale clears for 14 days before it moves from **On the way** to **Available now**.

## Where payouts go

Payouts are same-currency. A EUR balance pays to a EUR account and a USD balance pays to a USD account, so no currency conversion happens inside a payout.

## Schedule

Payouts run in two windows each month: the 1st and the 15th. On the **Payouts** tab, the **Payout schedule** section has two options:

- **Automatic**: "Paid automatically every window (1st & 15th)." Set a **Minimum payout**. Below that amount, AgentaOS holds your balance for the next window.
- **You ask**: "Queued for the next window when you request it." Click **Request payout**. The next window pays what is available at that time, including sales that clear before then.

A new account starts on **You ask**. The smallest payout is €50 or $50. When your balance is below that amount, it waits for a later window.

With a verified account and a bank account, your first payout goes out in the first window after all three are true: your first sales have cleared (14 days after each sale), your **Available now** amount is at least €50 or $50, and you have clicked **Request payout**. Money in **Reserve** is not paid out yet: the **Reserve** row shows the amount and, once it is set, the date it frees up.

## Fees

Each payout carries a **Payout fee** of 1% of the amount paid out, with a minimum of €1 or $1. The route adds its own cost:

- **Bank transfer fee**: what our banking partner charges for the transfer, passed through at cost. It depends on the country and currency. See the payout fee table on [Supported countries](/mor/supported-countries).
- **Wallet corridor fee**: for a stablecoin payout. See [Stablecoin payouts](/payouts/stablecoin-payouts#fees).

On **You ask**, the **Payouts** tab shows what you would receive today and the fees before you click **Request payout**. The payout statement lists each fee after the payout.

## Documents for each payout

Every payout gets two documents on the **Payouts** tab:

- **Payout statement**: "What was paid this run: the covered sales, our commission, the payout fee, the bank transfer fee, and what reached your bank." Use it to match the payout to your bank statement.
- **Self-billed invoice**: your supply to AgentaOS, one line per sale that the payout covers, with the fees deducted beneath. The total is what reached your account. It shows no VAT line: it records the value of your supply to AgentaOS (Aristokrates OÜ, Estonia).

Give both documents to your accountant. See [Payout statements and self-billed invoices](/payouts/statements) for each line and how to use the documents in your books.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which currencies can I be paid in?">
    EUR and USD. Each currency pays out to an account in the same currency.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why was my payout not sent?">
    Check four things. Your account must be verified. You must have a bank account in that currency, or a wallet that you chose for that currency. Your available balance must be at least the minimum payout. On **You ask**, you must request the payout.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I be paid to a bank account and a wallet?">
    Yes, per currency. For example, EUR can pay out to your bank and USD to a wallet. See [Stablecoin payouts](/payouts/stablecoin-payouts).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a dispute change my payout?">
    Yes. The money from a disputed sale moves to **Paused (disputed)** and is not paid out until the dispute ends.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
