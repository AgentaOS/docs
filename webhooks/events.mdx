AgentaOS sends 12 webhook event types, for checkouts, outbound sends, subscriptions, disputes, account verification, and the dashboard's test event. This page is the full list. Every event shares the same envelope: an `id`, a `type`, a `business`, and a `data` object whose shape depends on the type. See [Webhooks](/payments/webhooks) for how to register a URL and verify the signature before you trust any of this.

Amounts follow the [money model](/api-reference/introduction#money-model): `amount` is a string in currency units, and `amount_minor` is an integer in the smallest currency unit.

<Note>
The field names below are the literal snake_case keys AgentaOS `POST`s to your `webhookUrl`: parse these in any language. The SDK's `webhooks.verify()` (Node.js) returns the same fields camelCased: `link_id` becomes `linkId`, `session_id` becomes `sessionId`, `trial_end` becomes `trialEnd`, and so on.
</Note>

## When each event fires

Each event type below lists every trigger in the platform. [Which action fires which event](#which-action-fires-which-event) gives the same facts in the other direction, per action.

| Event | Fires when | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `checkout.session.completed` | A buyer pays a checkout by card, bank transfer or stablecoin. A free trial starts: `amount` is `"0"` and `trial` is `true`. A trial with a trial price starts: `amount` is the amount charged. A later subscription charge succeeds: a renewal, the first charge after a trial, or an upgrade's prorated charge. A subscriber credit pays a renewal in full: `amount` is `"0"`. | Fulfill the order or unlock access. |
| `subscription.created` | The subscription becomes `active` or `trialing` for the first time: the buyer pays the first period, or saves a card for a trial. | Store the subscription `id` with your user. |
| `subscription.renewed` | A charge after the first one succeeds: a renewal, the first charge after a trial, or an upgrade's prorated charge. A renewal that a subscriber credit pays also fires it. | Extend access to `current_period_end`. |
| `subscription.payment_failed` | The status becomes `past_due` or `unpaid`: a renewal charge fails, including the first charge after a trial. | Ask the subscriber to update their card. |
| `subscription.updated` | A cancel is scheduled. A scheduled cancel is resumed. An upgrade is applied. A downgrade is scheduled. A scheduled downgrade is reverted. A scheduled downgrade takes over at the period end. | Store the payload as the subscription's latest state. |
| `subscription.canceled` | The status becomes `canceled`: at the period end after a scheduled cancel, or when the card processor ends the subscription for another reason. | Remove access. |
| `dispute.created` | AgentaOS finds a new dispute on one of your sales. | Mark the order as disputed and reply to our dispute email with your evidence; AgentaOS answers the buyer's bank. |
| `dispute.closed` | A dispute closes in your favor. A lost dispute sends no event. | Clear the disputed mark on the order. |
| `send.completed` | An outbound stablecoin send that you started is confirmed. | Mark the payout as paid. |
| `send.failed` | An outbound stablecoin send that you started fails: the broadcast fails, for example on a network error or an insufficient balance, or the transaction reverts on-chain. | Retry the send. |
| `account.updated` | A business's verification changes, in live mode only. Its identity check is verified or declined. Our reviewers approve, reject, hold or resume the business, or ask for a change. Our reviewers verify or withdraw its identity by hand. The event goes to the business and to the platform that manages it. | Show the business its next step from `verification`. |
| `webhook.test` | You click **Send test event** in **Settings → Developers → Webhooks**. | Check that your endpoint verifies it. |

## Event envelope

<ResponseField name="id" type="string">
  The event ID. A paid checkout's id is `evt_` plus the sale's transaction id, a send's id is `evt_` plus `data.transaction_id`, and every other event gets a random id such as `evt_4e1b9a0c2d3f5a67`. An event keeps its `id` across delivery attempts and a retry from the dashboard. The SDK's `WebhookEvent` type does not declare `id` yet, so deduplicate on the keys in [Deduplication](#deduplication).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="type" type="string">
  One of `checkout.session.completed`, `send.completed`, `send.failed`, `subscription.created`, `subscription.renewed`, `subscription.payment_failed`, `subscription.updated`, `subscription.canceled`, `dispute.created`, `dispute.closed`, `account.updated`, or `webhook.test`. Switch on this to decide how to parse `data`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="business" type="string">
  The id of the business the event happened in. On your own events it is your own id. If you are a [platform](/connect/overview), your webhook also receives the events of every business you manage, and there `business` is your client's id. See [Platform webhooks](/connect/webhooks).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  Event-specific payload. See each event below.
</ResponseField>

## Which action fires which event

Events describe money and account state, not every API call. An action that changes nothing about a payment, a subscription or a business's verification sends no event. A "check" below is AgentaOS reading the current state from the card processor. While the buyer's checkout page is open, AgentaOS checks within seconds. A background check runs every 5 minutes for subscriptions and every 10 minutes for disputes.

## Which URL receives an event

A `checkout.session.completed` event goes to one URL: the checkout's own `webhookUrl` if it has one, else its payment link's, else your account's webhook URL. The first one found is the only one that receives it. Every other event goes to your account's webhook URL. If no URL applies, AgentaOS sends nothing and shows no error.

## Delivery and retries

AgentaOS sends each event as a `POST` with `Content-Type: application/json` and an `X-AgentaOS-Signature` header. A delivery follows these rules:

- Each attempt times out after 10 seconds. A response outside `2xx`, a timeout, or a network error counts as a failed attempt.
- AgentaOS makes up to 3 attempts. It waits 1 second before the second attempt and 2 seconds before the third.
- The 3 attempts carry the same signature, with the same `t`.
- AgentaOS does not follow redirects. A `3xx` response is a failed attempt.
- AgentaOS refuses a URL that resolves to a private or internal address, such as `localhost`, `10.0.0.0/8` or `192.168.0.0/16`, and marks the event failed without an attempt. For local development, expose your server through a public HTTPS tunnel.

After the third failed attempt, AgentaOS marks the event `failed` in **Settings → Developers → Webhooks** and sends it no more by itself. An owner or admin can send a failed event again with the retry button on its row (labeled "Retry webhook"). The retry sends the same body, signed again with a new `t`.

Events are delivered independently of each other. Two events about the same subscription can arrive in either order.

## Deduplication

The same event can arrive more than once: after a retry, or when your server stored the event but answered too slowly. Record a key per event and skip one you have already handled.

| Event | Key |
|---|---|
| `checkout.session.completed` | `data.session_id` |
| `send.completed`, `send.failed` | `data.transaction_id` |
| `subscription.created`, `subscription.renewed`, `subscription.payment_failed`, `subscription.canceled` | The event `type`, `data.id` and `data.current_period_end` |
| `subscription.updated` | None. Two updates in the same period, such as a cancel scheduled and then resumed, share the same `type`, `data.id` and `data.current_period_end`. Apply the event as the subscription's latest state instead of skipping it. |
| `dispute.created`, `dispute.closed` | The event `type` and `data.dispute_id` |

## Event order for subscriptions

AgentaOS checks each subscription with the card processor on a schedule and sends the events for what changed. The events below are what your endpoint receives. Unlock access on `checkout.session.completed`: it carries the `metadata` of the checkout, with your own ids.

| What happens | Events |
|---|---|
| A buyer subscribes and pays the first period | `subscription.created` and `checkout.session.completed`, in either order |
| A buyer starts a free trial | `subscription.created` with `status: "trialing"`, and `checkout.session.completed` with `amount: "0"` and `trial: true` |
| A buyer starts a trial with a trial price | `subscription.created`, and `checkout.session.completed` with the amount charged for the trial. There is no `trial` field. |
| The trial ends and the first full charge succeeds | `checkout.session.completed` and `subscription.renewed` |
| A later period is paid | `checkout.session.completed` and `subscription.renewed` |
| A renewal charge fails | `subscription.payment_failed` |
| A cancel is scheduled or resumed, or a plan changes | `subscription.updated` |
| The subscription ends | `subscription.canceled` |

The `checkout.session.completed` for a renewal, or for the first charge after a trial, has its own `session_id`. Its `metadata` is AgentaOS's own, not the metadata you set on the checkout, so your `metadata.customerId` is not on it. Match it to your user through `metadata.subscriptionId`, the subscription's `id`, which is also on the first checkout event. A renewal's `metadata` also has `subscriptionCycle: true`.

## `checkout.session.completed`

**Fires when:**

- A buyer pays a checkout by card, bank transfer or stablecoin.
- A free trial starts: `amount` is `"0"` and `trial` is `true`.
- A trial with a trial price starts: `amount` is the amount charged.
- A later subscription charge succeeds: a renewal, the first charge after a trial, or an upgrade's prorated charge.
- A subscriber credit pays a renewal in full: `amount` is `"0"`.

This is the event to listen for to fulfill an order. For the order of subscription events, see [Event order for subscriptions](#event-order-for-subscriptions).

<Expandable title="data fields" defaultOpen>
  <ResponseField name="link_id" type="string | null">
    The payment link's public token: the last part of its `checkoutUrl` (`https://app.agentaos.ai/pay/<token>`). It is not the link's `id`, which `checkouts.create({ linkId })` and the subscription events use. `null` for a checkout created without a link.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="session_id" type="string">
    The checkout's public session ID. Key your fulfillment and deduplication on it.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="amount" type="string">
    Amount paid, in currency units, as a string, e.g. `"49.99"`. `"0"` for a free trial start.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="currency" type="string">
    Settlement currency, e.g. `"EUR"`.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="rail" type="string">
    How the buyer paid: `card`, `sepa` (a bank transfer), or `crypto`.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="tx_hash" type="string | null">
    On-chain transaction hash for a stablecoin payment. `null` for a card or bank sale: use `vendor_reference` there.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="vendor_reference" type="string | null">
    The payment's reference at the card processor or the bank. `null` for a stablecoin payment.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="payer" type="string | null">
    The paying wallet address for a stablecoin payment. `null` for a card or bank sale.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="payer_type" type="string">
    `human` or `agent`: whether a person or an autonomous agent made the payment.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="customer_id" type="string | null">
    AgentaOS's own id for the buyer, the `id` the [Customers API](/sdk/pay-customers) returns. It is not your user id: put your own id in `metadata`, for example `metadata.customerId`.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="buyer_email" type="string | null">
    The buyer's email, when the checkout collected one.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="buyer_name" type="string | null">
    The buyer's name, when the checkout collected one.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="network" type="string">
    `card` for a card payment, `bank_transfer` for a bank transfer, or a CAIP-2 network ID such as `"eip155:8453"` (Base) for a stablecoin payment.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="livemode" type="boolean">
    `true` in live mode, `false` in test mode.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="overpaid_by_cents" type="number | null">
    Set when a bank-transfer buyer sent more than the amount due (beyond a 1-cent tolerance). `null` otherwise.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="trial" type="boolean">
    Present only when the checkout started a free trial, and then always `true`. `metadata.trial` is also `true` on that event.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="trial_end" type="string | null">
    Present only with `trial`: the ISO 8601 time the trial ends and the first charge is due.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="metadata" type="object">
    The key-value data you attached when you created the checkout or payment link. On a renewal it is AgentaOS's own: see [Event order for subscriptions](#event-order-for-subscriptions).
  </ResponseField>
</Expandable>

<Note>
The SDK's `CheckoutCompletedData` type differs from the wire body in two ways. It types `txHash`, `payer` and `linkId` as `string`, but they are `null` in the cases above, so check for `null`. It does not declare `rail`, `vendorReference`, `customerId`, `buyerEmail`, `buyerName`, `overpaidByCents`, `trial` or `trialEnd` yet, although `verify()` returns them: read them with a cast, as shown in [Payment SDK: Webhooks](/sdk/pay-webhooks#fields-the-type-does-not-declare-yet).
</Note>

## Subscription events

**Fires when:**

- **`subscription.created`**: the subscription becomes `active` or `trialing` for the first time: the buyer pays the first period, or saves a card for a trial.
- **`subscription.renewed`**: a charge after the first one succeeds: a renewal, the first charge after a trial, or an upgrade's prorated charge. A renewal that a subscriber credit pays also fires it. The first period does not fire it.
- **`subscription.payment_failed`**: the status becomes `past_due` or `unpaid`: a renewal charge fails, including the first charge after a trial.
- **`subscription.updated`**: a cancel is scheduled or resumed (`cancel_at_period_end`), an upgrade is applied (new `plan_name`, `link_id` and `amount_minor`), a downgrade is scheduled (`pending_plan` set), a scheduled downgrade is reverted (`pending_plan` null again), or a scheduled downgrade takes over at the period end.
- **`subscription.canceled`**: the status becomes `canceled`: at the period end after a scheduled cancel, or when the card processor ends the subscription for another reason. No more charges follow.

Subscription lifecycle events, delivered to your account's webhook URL. All five share the same `data` shape. For the order they arrive in, see [Event order for subscriptions](#event-order-for-subscriptions).

<Expandable title="data fields" defaultOpen>
  <ResponseField name="id" type="string">The subscription's id, a UUID. The same value as `metadata.subscriptionId` on the checkout event and `id` in the [subscriptions API](/sdk/pay-subscriptions).</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="status" type="string">The status after the change, one of the eight [subscription statuses](/sdk/pay-subscriptions#statuses). A subscription that never activated sends no event, so `incomplete` and `incomplete_expired` do not appear on events.</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="plan_name" type="string | null">Plan name, from the backing payment link.</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="currency" type="string">e.g. `"EUR"`.</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="amount_minor" type="number">Per-cycle price in integer minor units. `1000` = `€10.00`.</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="current_period_end" type="string | null">ISO 8601, or `null` before the first cycle.</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="cancel_at_period_end" type="boolean">`true` if cancellation is scheduled for period end.</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="link_id" type="string">The current plan's payment link `id`, a UUID: the `linkId` you pass to `checkouts.create`. It is not the token in the checkout URL that `checkout.session.completed` sends as `link_id`.</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="pending_plan" type="object | null">
    Null unless a downgrade is scheduled. Then `{ link_id, plan_name, amount_minor, effective_at }`: the plan the subscription switches to at `effective_at` (the period end). `plan_name`, `link_id` and `amount_minor` above stay what the buyer paid for until then.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="discount" type="object | null">
    Null unless the subscriber redeemed a discount code. Then `{ code, kind }`, where `kind` is `discount` or `tracking` (a code that changes no price). The amount the code took off is on the subscription's first invoice. The SDK's TypeScript types do not list this field yet.
  </ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="customer_email" type="string | null" />
  <ResponseField name="customer_name" type="string | null" />
  <ResponseField name="livemode" type="boolean">`true` in live mode, `false` in test mode.</ResponseField>
</Expandable>

  "type": "subscription.renewed",
  "business": "9a8b7c6d-5e4f-3a2b-1c0d-9e8f7a6b5c4d",
  "data": {
    "id": "b7e2a1c4-5d6f-4a8b-9c0d-1e2f3a4b5c6d",
    "status": "active",
    "plan_name": "Pro Monthly",
    "link_id": "4cbb8f52-7cf2-4480-8c66-34f794cd23d0",

## Dispute events

**Fires when:**

- **`dispute.created`**: AgentaOS finds a new dispute on one of your sales.
- **`dispute.closed`**: a dispute closes in your favor (`status` is `won`). A lost dispute sends no event.

A dispute starts when a buyer's bank opens a chargeback on a sale. Both events share the same `data` shape.

<Expandable title="data fields" defaultOpen>
  <ResponseField name="dispute_id" type="string">The dispute's id at the card processor.</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="status" type="string">The dispute's status as the card processor reports it, e.g. `needs_response` or `won`.</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="transaction_id" type="string">The sale the dispute is about.</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="amount_minor" type="number">Disputed amount in integer minor units. `4999` = `€49.99`.</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="currency" type="string">e.g. `"EUR"`.</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="livemode" type="boolean">`true` in live mode, `false` in test mode.</ResponseField>
</Expandable>

```json Example
{
  "id": "evt_1a2b3c4d5e6f7a8b",
  "type": "dispute.created",
  "business": "9a8b7c6d-5e4f-3a2b-1c0d-9e8f7a6b5c4d",
  "data": {
    "dispute_id": "dp_1P...",
    "status": "needs_response",
    "transaction_id": "8c7d6e5f-4a3b-2c1d-0e9f-8a7b6c5d4e3f",
    "amount_minor": 4999,
    "currency": "EUR",
    "livemode": true
  }
}
```

## `account.updated`

**Fires when:** a business's verification changes, in live mode only.

- Its identity check is verified or declined.
- Our reviewers approve, reject, hold or resume the business, or ask for a change.
- Our reviewers verify or withdraw its identity by hand.

The event goes to the business and to the platform that manages it.

The body is the state after the change.

<Expandable title="data fields" defaultOpen>
  <ResponseField name="verification" type="string">`unverified`, `in_review`, `verified`, `on_hold`, or `rejected`.</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="changes_requested" type="boolean">We asked the business for a change and are waiting on it.</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="identity_verified" type="boolean">The identity check is done. For another app of your own company, this is your company's check.</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="livemode" type="boolean">Always `true`: verification is about live money. The event still reaches your endpoint while you build in test mode, because an account has one webhook endpoint.</ResponseField>
</Expandable>

```json Example
{
  "id": "evt_4e1b9a0c2d3f5a67",
  "type": "account.updated",
  "business": "5b1f0c2e-8a4d-4c7b-9e21-3f6a7d8c9b10",
  "data": {
    "verification": "verified",
    "changes_requested": false,
    "identity_verified": true,
    "livemode": true
  }
}
```

## `webhook.test`

**Fires when:** you click **Send test event** in **Settings → Developers → Webhooks**.

Use it to check that your endpoint receives and verifies events. It goes to your account's webhook URL, signed like every other event.

<Expandable title="data fields" defaultOpen>
  <ResponseField name="message" type="string">Always `"Test event from AgentaOS"`.</ResponseField>
  <ResponseField name="livemode" type="boolean">`true` if you sent it from live mode, `false` from test mode.</ResponseField>
</Expandable>

```json Example
{
  "id": "evt_test_5c2e9a1f0b7d3e48",
  "type": "webhook.test",
  "business": "9a8b7c6d-5e4f-3a2b-1c0d-9e8f7a6b5c4d",
  "data": {
    "message": "Test event from AgentaOS",
    "livemode": false
  }
}
```
