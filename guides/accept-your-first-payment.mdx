This guide takes you from no product to a first sale in the dashboard. You create a product, pay for it yourself as a test buyer, and then find the sale, the invoice, and your net amount. Everything runs in **Test mode**, so no real money moves.

The full product settings are on the [Products](/payments/payment-links) page. The buyer's checkout, field by field, is on the [Checkouts](/payments/checkouts) page.

## Before you start

- An AgentaOS account. Sign up at [app.agentaos.ai](https://app.agentaos.ai). The dashboard opens in **Test mode**.
- The test card `4242 4242 4242 4242`, with any future expiry date and any CVC. Type it only into the card form on the checkout page.

## Create the product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    In the sidebar, click **Products**, then click **New product**. The form opens with the title **Create a new product**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Under **Details**, type a **Product name**, for example `Starter guide`, and a **Description**. The buyer sees both on the checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the price">
    Under **Pricing**, type the price, for example `19`, and pick **EUR** or **USD**. Leave **One-time** selected.

    Under the price, the form shows what a buyer pays with VAT. For a €19 product, it reads "EU consumers pay €23.56 (€19.00 + €4.56 VAT), you receive €19.00 before fees. Everyone else pays €19.00." Select **Prices include VAT** if your price already contains the VAT.

    <Frame caption="The form for a one-time product at €19. The line under the price shows what a buyer pays with VAT.">
      <img src="/images/product/new-product-form.png" alt="The Create a new product form. Details has the product name Starter guide and the description A 40-page PDF on pricing your first product. Pricing shows 19, EUR, and One-time, the Prices include VAT checkbox, and the line EU consumers pay €23.56 (€19.00 + €4.56 VAT), you receive €19.00 before fees. Everyone else pays €19.00. The Summary on the right shows Buyer pays €19.00." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create product**. The next screen says "Starter guide is live". Live here is the product's word for published. You are still in test mode.

    <Frame caption="The screen after you create the product, in test mode.">
      <img src="/images/product/product-created.png" alt="The confirmation screen: Starter guide is live. Pay it yourself first. It takes a minute and proves the whole thing works. Below are the button Try a test payment, the line Use card 4242 4242 4242 4242, any future date, any CVC. Nothing is charged, and the buttons Copy link and View product." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Share the buyer link

Click **View product** to open the product's page. Every product has one buyer link, and one link works for every buyer.

- **Copy link** copies the buyer link. Send it in an email or a chat, or put it behind a button on your site.
- **QR code** shows a code that opens the same link on a phone.
- **Preview** opens the checkout in a new tab, the way a buyer sees it.

</Frame>

## Pay it yourself

Click **Preview** on the product's page, or **Try a test payment** on the screen after you create the product. The checkout opens. A banner at the top reads **Test payment · 4242 4242 4242 4242 · any expiry · any CVC**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill in the buyer details">
    Type an email such as `buyer@example.com` and pick a **Country**. The order summary on the left adds the tax for that country and shows the **Total due**.

    <Frame caption="The first checkout step, with a buyer in Germany.">
      <img src="/images/product/first-payment-details.png" alt="The checkout for Starter guide with the Test payment banner at the top. The summary shows Starter guide €19.00, Tax 24% €4.56, and Total due €23.56, and Sold by AgentaOS, merchant of record. The form has the email buyer@example.com, the country Germany, and the button Continue to payment." />
    </Frame>

    The 24% is the Estonian rate. The checkout charges it to every EU consumer while AgentaOS sells under the EU distance-selling threshold. See [How tax works](/mor/tax#the-rate-on-eu-sales-today).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue to payment">
    Click **Continue to payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the test card">
    Type `4242 4242 4242 4242` in **Card number**, any future date in **Expiration date**, and any three digits in **Security code**.

    <Frame caption="The Review & pay step, before the card is entered.">
      <img src="/images/product/first-payment-review.png" alt="The Review and pay step: Paying as buyer@example.com, €23.56, Edit details, the Card and Bank transfer options, the card form with Card number, Expiration date, and Security code, Country Germany, and the button Pay €23.56." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Click **Pay** followed by the amount, here **Pay €23.56**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the buyer sees

The checkout shows **Payment complete** with the amount, the invoice number, and the lines of the invoice. In test mode, it also says "Test payment: no money was paid. This invoice and receipt are not valid." The buyer can click **Download invoice** or **Download receipt**.

<Frame caption="What the buyer sees after paying.">
  <img src="/images/product/checkout-success.png" alt="The Payment complete page: €23.56, Paid to AgencyOne, the notice Test payment: no money was paid. This invoice and receipt are not valid, the invoice TEST-INV-2026-0004 with Starter guide €19.00, Tax 24% €4.56, and Total €23.56, and the buttons Download invoice and Download receipt." />
</Frame>

The buyer also gets a receipt by email, with the invoice attached. The subject line is "Receipt from" followed by your business name. In test mode, the subject starts with `[TEST]`, and the invoice number starts with `TEST-`. See [Test mode and testing](/getting-started/test-mode#test-invoices-receipts-and-emails) for how each test document is marked.

## See the sale

<Steps>
  <Step title="Home">
    In the sidebar, click **Home**. The setup guide marks **Create your first product** as done: "Test payment cleared. Nice."

    <Frame caption="Home after the first test payment.">
      <img src="/images/product/home.png" alt="The Home page: Welcome, AgencyOne. The Setup guide shows 1 of 4, with Create your first product checked and the line Test payment cleared. Nice. Real money next: verify your business." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transactions">
    Click **Transactions**. The sale is at the top of the list as **Received** with the amount the buyer paid.

    <Frame caption="The Transactions list with a test card payment.">
      <img src="/images/product/transactions.png" alt="The Transactions page with one row: Received +€12.40, Customer, Card via AgentaOS, Clearing, Sep 28, 2026." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The sale page">
    Click the sale to open it. Under **Buyer & payment**, three rows show where the buyer's money goes:

    - **VAT** is the tax on the sale. AgentaOS collects and handles it.
    - **AgentaOS fee** is the fee for the sale. See [Pricing](/mor/pricing).
    - **Your net** is what is left for you.

    <Frame caption="The sale page. VAT, AgentaOS fee, and Your net are at the bottom of Buyer & payment.">
      <img src="/images/product/sale-page.png" alt="The sale page: Paid, €23.56, for A 40-page PDF on pricing your first product. Buyer and payment lists Email buyer@example.com, Country Germany, Method Visa 4242, VAT (24%) €4.56, AgentaOS fee €1.91, and Your net €17.09, with the buttons Request refund, View invoice, and View product." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invoices">
    Click **Invoices**. The sale's invoice is in the list as **Paid**, with the buyer's email. Open it and click **Download invoice**. The PDF of a test invoice has a **TEST** badge next to its title.

    <Frame caption="The Invoices list with the invoice for a test sale.">
      <img src="/images/product/invoices.png" alt="The Invoices page with one invoice: buyer@example.com, INV-2026-0001, Sep 28, 2026, €12.40, Paid." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
