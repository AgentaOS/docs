AgentaOS charges a fee on each payment you accept, plus an optional monthly plan that lowers your rate. Tax registration, collection, remittance and invoicing are included in that fee, because AgentaOS is your merchant of record. Each payout carries a payout fee of 1% (minimum €1 or $1); see [Payouts](/payouts/overview#fees). There are no setup costs.

## Plans

| Plan | Bank & stablecoin | Card | Monthly |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pay as you go** | 1.5% | 4.5% + €0.50 | €0 |
| **Pro** | 1.0% | 4.0% + €0.40 | €49 / mo |
| **Scale** | ~0.5% | Cost-plus (custom) | Custom |

- **Pay as you go** is the no-commitment entry lane. No monthly fee, slightly higher per-transaction rate.
- **Pro** lowers every rate by ~0.5%. It pays for itself at roughly €10,000/mo in volume, and the dashboard tells you when you cross that line.
- **Scale** is for high-volume merchants (higher limits, negotiated rates).

Your plan and rates are on **Settings → Billing**. To move to Pro, email [contact@agentaos.ai](mailto:contact@agentaos.ai) and AgentaOS switches your account. The new rate applies from your next payment.

[Platforms](/connect/overview), which manage businesses for clients or for other apps of the same company, work on every plan.

Transaction fees apply to the gross amount and are netted from your settlement, so there is nothing to invoice or pay separately.

## What a card payment costs

The card rate depends on where your buyer's card was issued. AgentaOS settles each charge in its own currency and lets the buyer cover FX, so you are not paying a conversion markup.

| Buyer's card | Surcharge on Pay as you go |
|---|---|
| EEA (Europe) | None |
| UK, US and other non-EEA | 1.5% on top of the card rate |

On Pay as you go, a card issued outside the EEA costs 6.0% + €0.50 in total. The Pro card rate (4.0% + €0.40) is a flat price to you regardless of your buyer's origin.

## Payouts

Each payout carries a payout fee of 1% of the amount paid out, with a minimum of €1 or $1. A bank payout also carries the cost of the bank transfer, passed through at cost. See the payout fee table on [Supported countries](/mor/supported-countries) and [Payouts](/payouts/overview#fees).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there a monthly fee?">
    Only on Pro (€49/mo) and Scale. Pay as you go has no monthly fee.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When is Pro worth it?">
    Pro lowers every rate by about 0.5%, so it pays for itself at roughly €10,000/mo in volume. Below that, Pay as you go is cheaper. The dashboard nudges you the moment Pro would save you money.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the fee cover tax compliance?">
    Yes. The transaction fee is what funds AgentaOS acting as your Merchant of Record: tax registration, collection, remittance, and invoicing. You do not pay separately for those.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does a payout cost?">
    A payout fee of 1% of the amount paid out, with a minimum of €1 or $1. A bank transfer cost, where the route has one, passes through at cost.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the card rate change by country?">
    On Pay as you go, a card issued outside the EEA adds 1.5% to the card rate. The Pro card rate (4.0% + €0.40) is flat to you. See [Supported countries](/mor/supported-countries).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
