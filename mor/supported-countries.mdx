AgentaOS accepts purchases from buyers in almost every country, and pays merchants out by bank transfer across the countries listed below. For how the money moves end to end, see [How it works](/mor/how-it-works).

<Info>
If your country is not in the payout list: bank payout coverage expands over time. In the meantime, [stablecoin payouts (public preview)](/payouts/stablecoin-payouts) pay your balance to a wallet in USDC or EURC. Ask us at [contact@agentaos.ai](mailto:contact@agentaos.ai) and we confirm your options. The exception is the short list of [countries we cannot pay out to](#countries-we-cannot-pay-out-to): no payout reaches them, by bank or by wallet.
</Info>

## Payout methods (your side)

Whichever method your buyer uses, your AgentaOS balance settles in **EUR or USD**, then pays out to your bank account:

<Card title="Bank transfer in EUR or USD" icon="building-columns" href="/payouts/overview">
  Available in every supported country below, through our banking partner: by SEPA where your bank takes it, otherwise by Swift. See [Payout fees](#payout-fees).
</Card>

### Payout fees

Each payout carries the [payout fee](/payouts/overview#fees). On top of it, AgentaOS passes on our banking partner's transfer cost at cost. Payouts are sent in EUR or USD only, and each payout takes the cheapest route our banking partner offers to your bank:

| Route | Banking partner cost per payout |
|---|---|
| SEPA, in EUR | 0.00 EUR |
| Local transfer in the United States, in USD | 1.13 USD |
| Swift in EUR, shared cost | 5.21 EUR Swift fee, plus the partner's transfer fee. The recipient's bank may deduct its own fees |
| Swift in EUR, fixed cost | 25.22 EUR Swift fee, plus the partner's transfer fee. The recipient receives the exact amount |
| Swift in USD | 6.15 USD Swift fee, or 7.24 to 35.00 USD where the partner expects correspondent bank fees, plus the partner's transfer fee |

The partner's transfer fee on a Swift payout was 0.48 EUR on a 1,000 EUR quote and 1.13 USD on both a 1,000 USD quote and a 4,000 USD example. Currency conversion, where it applies, is charged separately.

USD Swift fee where correspondent bank fees are expected, for some countries: Albania 13.16 USD, Bosnia and Herzegovina 13.22 USD, Montenegro 13.19 USD, North Macedonia 16.83 USD, Serbia 17.25 USD, Turkey 19.24 USD. Where the partner expects these fees and the country has no rate of its own, the fee is 21.71 USD. With the transfer fee, a USD payout to North Macedonia costs 16.83 + 1.13 = 17.96 USD.

These are our banking partner's prices and can change. Our banking partner picks shared or fixed cost on a EUR Swift payout; you cannot choose. The quoted cost shows on the Payouts tab before you request a payout and on the statement after it. Your own bank or an intermediary bank may add a charge on arrival, which AgentaOS cannot see.

### Marks in the table

- No mark: your EUR account is paid by SEPA, at no partner cost. In the United States, your USD account is paid by local transfer.
- `†` No SEPA route. A EUR or USD payout travels by Swift, so the Swift cost in the table above applies.
- `‡` Swift in USD only. Our banking partner does not send EUR by Swift to this country, so you need a USD account.

A bank in a SEPA country that does not take SEPA transfers is paid by Swift instead. A USD payout to a SEPA country also travels by Swift. Three USD routes skip Swift: USD to Hong Kong goes through the local CHATS system for 7.97 USD, USD to a DBS account in Singapore costs 1.49 USD, and USD to a personal PrivatBank account in Ukraine costs 1.13 USD plus 1.94%.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I sell to buyers in any country?">
    Yes, through the card processor's global network. Purchases are blocked only in the sanctioned or embargoed jurisdictions listed above.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What currency lands in my balance?">
    Your AgentaOS balance always settles in EUR or USD, whatever currency your buyer paid in and whatever method they used.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a European bank account to get paid?">
    No. Bank payouts reach the countries listed above, and coverage expands over time. See [Payouts](/payouts/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My country is not on the payout list. What now?">
    Bank payout coverage expands over time as our banking partner adds routes. Ask us at [contact@agentaos.ai](mailto:contact@agentaos.ai) and we confirm your options.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
