AgentaOS accepts purchases from buyers in almost every country, and pays merchants out by bank transfer across the countries listed below. For how the money moves end to end, see [How it works](/mor/how-it-works).

<Info>
If your country is not in the payout list: bank payout coverage expands over time. In the meantime, [stablecoin payouts (public preview)](/payouts/stablecoin-payouts) pay your balance to a wallet in USDC or EURC. Ask us at [contact@agentaos.ai](mailto:contact@agentaos.ai) and we confirm your options. The exception is the short list of [countries we cannot pay out to](#countries-we-cannot-pay-out-to): no payout reaches them, by bank or by wallet.
</Info>

## Payout methods (your side)

Whichever method your buyer uses, your AgentaOS balance settles in **EUR or USD**, then pays out to your bank account:

<Card title="Local bank transfer" icon="building-columns" href="/payouts/overview">
  Available across every supported country below, through our banking partner. See [Payout fees](#payout-fees).
</Card>

### Payout fees

Each payout carries the [payout fee](/payouts/overview#fees). AgentaOS adds the bank transfer cost at cost, and that cost depends on the route your money takes:

| Route | Fee per payout |
|---|---|
| Local bank transfer, including SEPA | A small flat fee |
| Swift in EUR, shared cost | 5.21 EUR, and the recipient's bank may deduct its own fees |
| Swift in EUR, fixed cost | 25.22 EUR, and the recipient receives the exact amount |
| Swift in USD, no correspondent fees expected | 6.15 USD |
| Swift in USD, correspondent fees expected | 7.24 to 35.00 USD by country, and the recipient receives the exact amount |

Our banking partner's own transfer fee comes on top of the Swift fee: in its published example, 1.13 USD on a 4,000 USD Swift transfer. Currency conversion, where it applies, is charged separately. Swift fee in USD by country, where correspondent fees are expected: Bosnia and Herzegovina 13.22 USD, Montenegro 13.19 USD, Albania 13.16 USD, North Macedonia 16.83 USD, Serbia 17.25 USD, Turkey 19.24 USD. A country without its own rate costs 21.71 USD. These are our banking partner's prices and can change.

Our banking partner picks the route for your account; you cannot choose between the shared and the fixed cost. The quoted cost shows on the Payouts tab before you request a payout and on the statement after it. Your own bank or an intermediary bank may add a charge on arrival, which AgentaOS cannot see.

### Marks in the table

- `**` The local route pays personal bank accounts only, in the recipient's own name. A business account in these countries is paid by Swift instead, so the cross-border fee in the table above applies.
- `*` China: a business is paid to a CNY account at a bank in mainland China, with a transfer reason on file; the recipient's bank can ask for the contract and the invoice. A person is paid through a card or wallet network, with a cap on each payout.
- `†` No local route. The payout travels by Swift, so the cross-border fee in the table above applies.

Albania, Moldova, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Serbia are paid in EUR by SEPA transfer, the same local route as the euro area. A bank there that does not take SEPA transfers is paid by Swift instead, so the cross-border fee applies.

Some routes cap one payout. Brazil: 10,000 USD to a business account and 3,000 USD to a personal account, and the account holder's tax number is required. Argentina: 18,000 USD per calendar month. India: 1.5 million INR per working day. Ukraine: 29,999 UAH per transfer, except to PrivatBank. These caps are our banking partner's and can change. If your balance is above a cap, ask us before the payout window.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I sell to buyers in any country?">
    Yes, through the card processor's global network. Purchases are blocked only in the sanctioned or embargoed jurisdictions listed above.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What currency lands in my balance?">
    Your AgentaOS balance always settles in EUR or USD, whatever currency your buyer paid in and whatever method they used.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a European bank account to get paid?">
    No. Bank payouts reach the countries listed above, and coverage expands over time. See [Payouts](/payouts/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My country is not on the payout list. What now?">
    Bank payout coverage expands over time as our banking partner adds routes. Ask us at [contact@agentaos.ai](mailto:contact@agentaos.ai) and we confirm your options.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
