# Activate your EUR account (Individual)

A real European IBAN in your name, linked to your AgentaOS wallet.

<Note>
  Takes around **10 minutes** to fill in. Approval usually arrives within **one business day**.
</Note>

## Before you start

<Card title="Have these ready" icon="clipboard-list">
  - **One photo ID**: passport, national ID card, or residence permit. Must be valid (not expired).
  - **Proof of address**: utility bill, government letter, or a statement from a traditional bank. Must show your full name and current address, dated within the last 3 months.
  - **Good lighting and your phone or webcam**: you'll take a live selfie at the end.
  - **Age 18 or older**: the EUR account is only available to adults.
</Card>

<Warning>
  Statements from **neobanks and digital-only banks are not accepted** as proof of address. Use a utility bill or a government letter instead.
</Warning>

## Step-by-step

<Steps>

<Step title="Start in AgentaOS">
  Open **Payouts** from the sidebar and click **Activate EUR account**. This is where SEPA payouts will land once the account is approved.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/banking/01-activate.png" alt="AgentaOS Payouts page with the Activate EUR account button" />
  </Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Open the account">
  A short consent screen explains what comes next. Click **Open the account**.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/banking/02-open-account.png" alt="Consent screen explaining what is needed to open the EUR account" />
  </Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Choose Sign up">
  First time? Click **Sign up free**. Already have an account? Choose **Login**.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/banking/03-create-account.png" alt="Sign up or Login choice" />
  </Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Create your login">
  Enter your **email**, choose a **password**, and pick your **country** of residence. Tick both agreement boxes and click **Sign up**.

  <Tip>
    **Use the same email you used for your AgentaOS account.** It keeps everything linked under one identity and makes future sign-ins simpler. Approval status and your IBAN details will arrive there too.
  </Tip>

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/banking/04-signup.png" alt="Email, password and country form" />
  </Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Get started">
  A short welcome screen kicks off the document check. Answer a couple of quick yes-or-no questions, tick the agreement box, and click **Continue**.

  From here you'll upload your ID, do a live selfie, and add your proof of address. Most people finish in 5 to 10 minutes.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/banking/05-verification.png" alt="Get started screen before the document check" />
  </Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Identity check">
  Upload one photo ID. Pick the type that's easiest for you: passport, national ID card, or residence permit.

  Make sure every corner of the document is visible and there's no glare on the photo or text.
</Step>

<Step title="Live selfie">
  Your camera will guide you through a short liveness check. Look straight at the camera and follow the on-screen instructions.

  <Tip>
    Find a well-lit spot before you start. Avoid backlight from a window behind you. Glasses and hats can cause retries.
  </Tip>
</Step>

<Step title="Proof of address">
  Upload a document that shows your full name and current address, dated within the last 3 months.

  **Accepted documents:**
  - Utility bill (electricity, water, internet, gas)
  - Government document (tax statement, residency certificate)
  - Bank statement from a traditional bank in your country
  - Credit card statement from a traditional bank in your country

  <Warning>
    Statements from **neobanks and digital-only banks are not accepted**. If your main bank is fully online, use a utility bill or a government letter instead.
  </Warning>
</Step>

</Steps>

## Tips to avoid delays

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Proof of address older than 3 months">
    The single most common cause of rejection. Pull a fresh one before you start.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Wrong name on the address proof">
    The name has to match your photo ID exactly. Bills in a partner's or family member's name are not accepted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Selfie attempts skipped or interrupted">
    If the camera prompts you to retry, finish the retry before closing the page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cut-off documents">
    Capture the full page. Edges, dates, and reference numbers all need to be visible.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Politically exposed status">
    If you're a senior public official, hold elected office, or are close family of one, you'll be asked for an extra source-of-funds document. Have it ready to keep things moving.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What happens next

Most reviews finish within one business day. Once approved, your EUR IBAN appears on your **Payouts** page and SEPA transfers start landing in your AgentaOS account.
