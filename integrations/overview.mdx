## Add your tool

If your product works with AgentaOS (email, affiliates, analytics, starter kits, no-code builders or anything else), you can publish a guide here.

1. Fork [AgentaOS/docs](https://github.com/AgentaOS/docs) and add `integrations/<your-tool>.mdx`.
2. Cover: what the integration does, setup steps, the AgentaOS webhook events or API calls it uses, and a link to your own docs.
3. Add the page to the **Integrations** group in `docs.json` and open a pull request.

We review guides for accuracy and keep the wording factual. Questions: [contact@agentaos.ai](mailto:contact@agentaos.ai).
