A checkout is one attempt to collect one payment. Each time a buyer opens a product's buyer link, AgentaOS creates a new checkout for that buyer. You can also create a checkout from your server when your app already knows who the buyer is. In both cases the buyer pays on the same hosted page at `https://app.agentaos.ai/…`.

The **Checkouts** tab on the Products page lists every checkout with its status: **Open**, **Completed**, or **Expired**.

## The buyer's checkout

The hosted checkout has two steps. The order summary stays on the left: the product, the subtotal, the tax, and the total due. Under the summary, the page states **Sold by AgentaOS, merchant of record.**

### Step 1: Your details

<Frame caption="The Your details step. The country sets the tax, and a business buyer can add a VAT ID.">
  <img src="/images/product/hosted-checkout-details.png" alt="The hosted checkout's Your details step for Plan A at 10 euros plus 24 percent tax, with email, full name, country, the Add VAT ID link, the discount code field, and Continue to payment" />
</Frame>

The buyer fills in:

- **Email**, required. The receipt goes to this address.
- **Full name**, marked **Optional**.
- **Country**, required. The hint under it reads "Used to calculate tax on this purchase." After the buyer picks a country, the hint shows the new total, such as "Total updates to €12.40 including tax." For a country outside the scope of VAT, it reads "No tax for this country. Total stays the list price."
- **Buying for a business? Add VAT ID**, a link that opens the VAT ID field. A valid EU VAT ID from another EU country is checked live, and reverse charge (0% VAT) applies only after that check passes.
- **Discount code**, optional. See [Discount codes](/payments/discount-codes).

If the product has [custom checkout fields](/payments/payment-links#custom-checkout-fields), they appear under **Asked by** followed by your business name. The buyer then clicks **Continue to payment**.

### Step 2: Review & pay

<Frame caption="The Review & pay step. The order summary on the left states Sold by AgentaOS, merchant of record.">
  <img src="/images/product/hosted-checkout-review.png" alt="The hosted checkout's Review and pay step with Paying as, the Card and Bank transfer options, the card form, and the Pay 12.40 euros button" />
</Frame>

The top of the step shows **Paying as** with the buyer's email and the total. The buyer can open it to check the country, VAT ID, and tax, or click **Edit details** to go back. Under **Card details**, the payment form offers **Card** and, for EUR payments, **Bank transfer**. The buyer clicks **Pay** followed by the amount. Apple Pay and Google Pay appear above the form on devices that support them. The buyer types card details into the secure card form; your server and ours never receive them.

After the payment clears, the buyer gets a receipt by email. If the product has a Success URL, the checkout sends the buyer there.

### Test mode

A test-mode checkout shows a banner at the top: **Test payment · 4242 4242 4242 4242 · any expiry · any CVC**. Click the card number to copy it, then paste it into the card field. See [Test mode and testing](/getting-started/test-mode).

## Create a checkout from your server

Sharing the buyer link is enough for most sales. Create a checkout from your server when:

- The buyer is logged in to your app, and you must know which of your users paid.
- You want to attach your own data, such as a user ID or an order number, with `metadata`. The data comes back in the [webhook](/payments/webhooks).
- You already have the buyer's email or country and want to fill them in.

Pass the product's ID as `linkId`. On the product page, open **Building this into your site?** and click **Copy ID**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="SDK">
    ```typescript
    import { AgentaOS } from '@agentaos/pay';

    const agentaos = new AgentaOS(process.env.AGENTAOS_API_KEY!);

    const checkout = await agentaos.checkouts.create({
      linkId: 'link-uuid',
      buyerEmail: 'buyer@example.com',
      metadata: { userId: 'user_123' },
    });

    res.redirect(checkout.checkoutUrl);
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="cURL">
    ```bash
    curl -X POST https://api.agentaos.ai/api/v1/gateway/sessions \
      -H "x-api-key: sk_live_..." \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{
        "linkId": "link-uuid",
        "buyerEmail": "buyer@example.com",
        "metadata": { "userId": "user_123" }
      }'
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

A checkout created from a product takes the product's amount, currency, description, and URLs. Set `amountOverride` to charge a different amount on this checkout only.

<Warning>
Do not treat the Success URL redirect as proof of payment. The buyer can close the browser before the redirect. Use [webhooks](/payments/webhooks) or the checkout's `status` to confirm the payment.
</Warning>

### Create a checkout without a product

When your server knows the amount, you can create a checkout without a product. This checkout is always a one-time payment. To sell a subscription, use a subscription product and pass its `linkId`.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="SDK">
    ```typescript
    const checkout = await agentaos.checkouts.create({
      amount: 49.99,
      currency: 'EUR',
      description: 'Order #123',
      successUrl: 'https://myshop.com/success',
    });
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="CLI">
    ```bash
    agenta pay checkout --amount 49.99 --currency EUR --description "Order #123"
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Fill in the buyer's details

```typescript
const checkout = await agentaos.checkouts.create({
  linkId: 'link-uuid',
  buyerEmail: 'buyer@example.com',
  buyerName: 'Jane Doe',
  buyerCompany: 'Acme GmbH',
  buyerCountry: 'DE',
  buyerVat: 'DE123456789',
  buyerAddress: 'Hauptstrasse 1, Berlin',
});
```

The company, VAT number, and address are printed on the invoice. The buyer cannot change a field you fill in: the checkout marks it **Set by** followed by your business name. A field you leave out stays editable. If you do not set `buyerCountry`, the buyer picks the country, and that choice sets the VAT.

### Retrieve, list, and cancel

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="SDK">
    ```typescript
    const checkout = await agentaos.checkouts.retrieve('mZrESFyR7RC9RPsJfZCVkg');
    console.log(checkout.status); // 'open' | 'completed' | 'expired' | 'cancelled'

    const page = await agentaos.checkouts.list({ status: 'completed', limit: 10 });

    await agentaos.checkouts.cancel('mZrESFyR7RC9RPsJfZCVkg');
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="CLI">
    ```bash
    agenta pay get mZrESFyR7RC9RPsJfZCVkg
    agenta pay list --status completed --limit 10
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="cURL">
    ```bash
    curl https://api.agentaos.ai/api/v1/gateway/sessions/mZrESFyR7RC9RPsJfZCVkg \
      -H "x-api-key: sk_live_..."

    curl "https://api.agentaos.ai/api/v1/gateway/sessions?status=completed&limit=10" \
      -H "x-api-key: sk_live_..."

    curl -X POST https://api.agentaos.ai/api/v1/gateway/sessions/mZrESFyR7RC9RPsJfZCVkg/cancel \
      -H "x-api-key: sk_live_..."
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

An open checkout expires after `expiresIn` seconds: 1800 by default, from 300 to 86400. Canceling stops the buyer from paying. A payment that cleared before the cancel still completes.

The full parameter and response reference is in [Checkouts (SDK)](/sdk/pay-checkouts) and [agenta pay](/cli/pay).

## Money model

A plain `amount` is in currency units (`49.99` means €49.99), and a field whose name ends in `Minor` is an integer in minor units. The full rules are in the [money model](/api-reference/introduction#money-model).
