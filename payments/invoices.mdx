Every sale gets an invoice. You do not create it or send it; AgentaOS issues it when the payment clears. For a card or bank sale, AgentaOS is the merchant of record, so the invoice names AgentaOS (Aristokrates OÜ, Estonia) as the seller, "Merchant of Record for" your business. The invoice carries the buyer's details and the tax breakdown for the buyer's country.

You can also create an invoice by hand and email it to a customer. The customer pays it on the same hosted checkout as a product.

## The Invoices page

<Frame caption="The Invoices page. The tabs split invoices by status.">
  <img src="/images/product/invoices.png" alt="The Invoices page with the All, Open, Paid, and Void tabs and one paid invoice, INV-2026-0001, for 12.40 euros" />
</Frame>

In the sidebar, click **Invoices**. The page has four tabs, each with a count:

- **All**: every invoice, newest first.
- **Open**: invoices sent and waiting for payment. An open invoice past its due date shows **Overdue**.
- **Paid**: paid invoices for a period you pick, with **Revenue**, **VAT**, and **Invoices** totals at the top. A paid invoice with a chargeback shows **Disputed** or **Charged back**.
- **Void**: voided invoices, kept for your records.

The **How invoicing works** link at the top opens this page.

## When an invoice is issued

A sale from a product's buyer link gets its invoice when the payment clears. The invoice goes straight to **Paid**.

An invoice you create by hand, or a checkout your server creates, gets its invoice when you create it. That invoice sits in **Open** until the customer pays, then moves to **Paid**.

When the payment clears, AgentaOS also emails the buyer a receipt. See [Receipts](/payments/receipts).

## Create and send an invoice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new invoice">
    On the Invoices page, click **Create invoice**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Bill to">
    Enter the customer's name, email, and country. Add their VAT number if they are a business. The country sets the VAT.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add line items">
    Enter an item description, a quantity, and a price. Click **Add line item** for each extra line. Add **Notes** for the customer if you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the invoice settings">
    Under **Invoice settings**, choose the **Currency** and the **Payment terms**: **Net 7**, **Net 14**, **Net 30**, or **Due on receipt**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and send">
    Click **Review & send**. Check the email under **Delivery**, then click **Send to** followed by the customer's name. To share the link yourself instead, click **Create without sending**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The builder saves your draft as you type. After you create the invoice, the next screen has **Copy payment link** and **Download invoice**. The customer pays on the hosted checkout, by card or, for EUR invoices, by bank transfer. You can also start an invoice from a customer's row on the [Customers](/payments/customers) page.

## Download a PDF, a statement, or a CSV

- **Invoice PDF**: the seller and buyer details, the line items, and the tax breakdown (subtotal, tax, total).
- **Statement**: on the **Paid** tab, click **Download statement**. The PDF covers the period you picked, with the opening and closing figures, every transaction, and a VAT summary by rate.
- **CSV**: on the **Paid** tab, click **Export CSV**. The file has 23 columns for accounting software, including the invoice number, the tax name, rate, and amount, a `total_eur` column, and the buyer's name, company, country, and VAT number.

## Void an invoice

Voiding marks the invoice as void in your records. It does not refund the buyer or touch the payment. Use it to correct a bookkeeping mistake. Only an owner or an admin can void an invoice.

On the **Paid** tab, click the void icon in the invoice's row, then click **Confirm void**. To cancel an open invoice that the customer will not pay, void it through the API (below).

<Warning>
You cannot undo a void. The invoice stays in the **Void** tab.
</Warning>

## From code

Every invoice on the Invoices page is also available through the API. The full reference is in [Invoices (SDK)](/sdk/pay-invoices) and [agenta invoices](/cli/invoices). Amounts on an invoice are in currency units: `19.99` means €19.99.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="SDK">
    ```typescript
    import { writeFileSync } from 'node:fs';
    import { AgentaOS } from '@agentaos/pay';

    const agentaos = new AgentaOS(process.env.AGENTAOS_API_KEY!);

    // List, newest first. Filter by date range and status.
    const page = await agentaos.invoices.list({ from: '2026-03-01', to: '2026-03-31', limit: 50 });

    const invoice = await agentaos.invoices.retrieve('invoice-uuid');

    writeFileSync('invoice.pdf', await agentaos.invoices.downloadPdf('invoice-uuid'));

    writeFileSync(
      'march-statement.pdf',
      await agentaos.invoices.downloadStatement({ from: '2026-03-01', to: '2026-03-31' }),
    );

    writeFileSync(
      'invoices.csv',
      await agentaos.invoices.exportCsv({ from: '2026-03-01', to: '2026-03-31' }),
    );

    await agentaos.invoices.void('invoice-uuid');
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="CLI">
    ```bash
    agenta invoices list --limit 50
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="cURL">
    ```bash
    curl "https://api.agentaos.ai/api/v1/gateway/invoices?from=2026-03-01&to=2026-03-31&status=paid" \
      -H "x-api-key: sk_live_..."

    curl https://api.agentaos.ai/api/v1/gateway/invoices/invoice-uuid/pdf \
      -H "x-api-key: sk_live_..." -o invoice.pdf

    curl "https://api.agentaos.ai/api/v1/gateway/invoices/statement?from=2026-03-01&to=2026-03-31" \
      -H "x-api-key: sk_live_..." -o statement.pdf

    curl "https://api.agentaos.ai/api/v1/gateway/invoices/export?from=2026-03-01&to=2026-03-31" \
      -H "x-api-key: sk_live_..." -o invoices.csv

    curl -X POST https://api.agentaos.ai/api/v1/gateway/invoices/invoice-uuid/void \
      -H "x-api-key: sk_live_..."
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The `status` filter takes `all`, `issued`, `paid`, or `voided`. An invoice's `status` is `issued` (the **Open** tab), `paid`, or `voided`. A list call returns `{ items, total, hasMore }`.
