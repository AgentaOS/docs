<Update label="September 28, 2026" tags={["Checkout", "Platforms", "Payouts"]}>
  ## Embedded checkout

  Put the AgentaOS checkout on your own page, inline or as an overlay, with a script tag or the `@agentaos/checkout` package. Add your sites under **Approved sites**, and set the button color, font, corners, and background under **Checkout appearance**. It applies to the hosted checkout too. See [Embedded checkout](/payments/embedded-checkout).

  ## Platforms, in private preview

  Run your clients' businesses from one account. Add a business, invite your client by email, and take a share of each sale, counted on the price before VAT. From code, send the `AgentaOS-Account` header to act as a business you manage. Your webhook receives your clients' events, every event names its `business`, and `account.updated` tells you when a business's verification changes. See [Build a platform](/guides/build-a-platform).

  ## Stablecoin payouts, in public preview

  Be paid in USDC or EURC to a wallet instead of your bank account. See [Stablecoin payouts](/payouts/stablecoin-payouts).

  ## New pages

  [Payout statements and self-billed invoices](/payouts/statements), and [Product release phases](/getting-started/release-phases), which explains the Private preview and Public preview tags.
</Update>

<Update label="September 17, 2026" tags={["Checkout"]}>
  ## Discount codes

  Create a code under **Discounts**, and a buyer types it at checkout to pay less. A code can also take nothing off and only track who sent the buyer. See [Discount codes](/payments/discount-codes).
</Update>

<Update label="September 8, 2026" tags={["Subscriptions"]}>
  ## Downgrades at the period end

  A downgrade keeps the plan the buyer paid for until the end of the period, then switches. Pick the current plan again before then to revert the change. See [Subscriptions](/payments/subscriptions).
</Update>

<Update label="August 23, 2026" tags={["Subscriptions"]}>
  ## Free trials

  A subscription plan can start with a free trial.

  ## Plan changes

  Move a subscriber to another plan. An upgrade applies at once, and the buyer pays the prorated difference today. See [Subscriptions](/payments/subscriptions).
</Update>
