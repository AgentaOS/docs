New to payments? These are the terms that show up across the docs, in one place.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Merchant of record (MoR)">
    The legal seller of your product. Your buyer pays AgentaOS, not you, so AgentaOS collects the money, charges and remits the tax, issues the invoice, and pays you out. AgentaOS carries the tax liability as the legal seller. A lost dispute costs you the sale plus the dispute fee: see [Refunds and disputes](/mor/refunds-and-disputes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Destination VAT">
    VAT charged at the rate of the **buyer's** country, not yours. While AgentaOS sells under the EU distance-selling threshold, the checkout charges EU consumers the Estonian rate instead. See [How tax works](/mor/tax#the-rate-on-eu-sales-today).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="OSS (One-Stop Shop)">
    The EU scheme that lets one registration cover VAT across all EU countries. AgentaOS does not use OSS: it sells under the EU distance-selling threshold from its registration in Estonia. Either way, you never register in each country you sell to.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reverse charge">
    For a B2B sale to a VAT-registered business in another EU country, VAT shifts to the buyer, so the invoice shows no VAT. A buyer who enters a valid EU VAT ID at checkout is handled this way.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Product">
    The thing you sell, with its price. The API calls a product a payment link; there is no separate `product` API. The product's API ID is the `linkId`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="linkId">
    The payment link UUID (`paymentLinks.id`). Pass it to `checkouts.create({ linkId })`. On the product page, open **Building this into your site?** and click **Copy ID**. It is not the `/pay/…` URL and not the checkout `sessionId`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Buyer link">
    The public `/pay/…` URL of one product, which every buyer can open. In the API it is the payment link's `checkoutUrl`. The `id` / `linkId` is for the API.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Checkout">
    A single attempt to collect one payment. Created on its own or from a product with `checkouts.create({ linkId })`. Every checkout has a `checkoutUrl` you send the buyer to (`/checkout/{sessionId}`).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Subscription">
    A recurring charge on a card on file, billed automatically every cycle until canceled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Invoice vs. receipt">
    An **invoice** is the tax document for a sale (seller, buyer, VAT breakdown), issued automatically on every payment. A **receipt** is the emailed confirmation the buyer gets that they paid.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Settlement">
    The behind-the-scenes step where a completed payment becomes money in your balance, after the card or bank network clears it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Balance states: Available now, On the way, Reserve">
    **Available now** is cleared money that the next payout sends. **On the way** is sales that are still clearing, for 14 days after each sale, with the date each amount becomes available. **Reserve** is a safety hold on new accounts to cover refunds and chargebacks; the row shows the date it frees up. See [Payouts](/payouts/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reserve">
    A portion of your balance held temporarily on newer accounts to cover potential refunds or chargebacks. It releases automatically.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Payout">
    Money moving from your **Available now** balance to your bank account. Payouts go out in two windows each month, the 1st and the 15th. A new account starts on **You ask**: click **Request payout** and the next window pays it. Choose **Automatic** to be paid every window. See [Payouts](/payouts/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Chargeback (dispute)">
    When a buyer's bank reverses a card payment. While the dispute is open, the sale's money shows in **Paused (disputed)** on your balance and is not paid out. See [Refunds and disputes](/mor/refunds-and-disputes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Test mode vs. live mode">
    **Test mode** uses fake money and test cards, safe to explore, no verification needed. **Live mode** takes real money and unlocks after business verification.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Webhook">
    A signed HTTP call AgentaOS makes to your server when something happens (like a completed checkout), so your app does not have to keep asking.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
