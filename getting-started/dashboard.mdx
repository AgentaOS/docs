[app.agentaos.ai](https://app.agentaos.ai) is where you run your sales: you create products, follow the money, send invoices, and manage your team and API keys. This page walks through the sidebar from top to bottom.

The **Test** / **Live** switch at the bottom of the sidebar decides which data every page shows. Test mode is free to explore and processes no real payments. Live mode handles real money and opens after your business is verified. See [Test mode and live mode](/getting-started/test-mode).

## Home

Home is the page you see after you sign in. The **Setup guide** lists four steps to your first real sale: **Create your first product**, **Verify your business**, **Add a payout account**, and **Go live**. Each step shows a check mark when it is done. **New product** at the top right opens the product form.

  <img src="/images/product/home.png" alt="The Home page with the Setup guide: Create your first product is done, and Verify your business, Add a payout account, and Go live are next" />

Below the setup guide, Home shows tiles for **Revenue**, **Sales**, **Customers**, **Active subs**, and **MRR**, and a list of your recent activity.

## Go live

**Go live** tracks what is left before you can take real payments. The chip next to it counts the steps done, such as **1/4**. The main step is to verify your business. See [Go live](/guides/go-live) and [Account review](/mor/account-review).

## Products

**Products** lists everything you sell. Each product has a price, one-time or subscription, and a buyer link you can copy from its row. The **Checkouts** tab lists each checkout a buyer started. See [Products and payment links](/payments/payment-links).

  <img src="/images/product/products.png" alt="The Products and Services page with revenue, payments, and active products at the top and three products below" />
</Frame>

## Discounts

**Discounts** holds the codes a buyer can type at checkout for a lower price. See [Discount codes](/payments/discount-codes).

## Transactions

**Transactions** lists every payment and payout on your account. Search by name, amount, or ID. Filter by period, by **Payments** or **Payouts**, and by currency. **Export CSV** downloads the list.

<Frame caption="The Transactions page.">
  <img src="/images/product/transactions.png" alt="The Transactions page with search, period, type, and currency filters, the Export CSV button, and one received card payment of 12.40 euros" />
</Frame>

## Balances

**Balances** shows your money in EUR and USD. It has three tabs:

- **Overview**: your balance, split into **Available now**, **On the way**, and **Reserve**, with the **Total** at the bottom.
- **Payouts**: the payouts sent to you.
- **Payout accounts**: the bank accounts and wallets that receive your payouts.

<Frame caption="The Balances overview.">
  <img src="/images/product/balances.png" alt="The Balances page on the Overview tab with the balance split into Available now, On the way, and Reserve, and a Total at the bottom" />
</Frame>

See [Payouts](/payouts/overview) and [Payout accounts](/payouts/payout-accounts).

## Invoices

**Invoices** lists every invoice in four tabs: **All**, **Open**, **Paid**, and **Void**. Every sale gets an invoice automatically, and **Create invoice** bills a customer by hand. See [Invoices](/payments/invoices).

## Subscriptions

**Subscriptions** lists the subscriptions your buyers started by paying a subscription product. From here you can change a plan or cancel. See [Subscriptions](/payments/subscriptions).

## Customers

**Customers** lists everyone who paid you or whom you billed, with their revenue and number of payments. See [Customers](/payments/customers).

## Developers, Support, and Guides

**Developers** opens a menu with links to **AI agents**, **API keys**, and **Webhooks**, and to the docs, the API reference, the SDK, the CLI, and the MCP server. **Support** opens an email to the AgentaOS team. **Guides** opens these docs.

## Settings

**Settings** has five tabs:

  <Card title="Profile" icon="user">
    Your sign-in email and your passkey.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Business" icon="building">
    Your business details, the logo on invoices and pay pages, and whether prices include VAT.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Team" icon="users">
    Invite teammates to your account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Developers" icon="code">
    API keys, webhooks, and the sites allowed to show your embedded checkout.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Billing" icon="receipt">
    Your plan and the fee for each payment method.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

API keys belong to the mode you are in: a test key works only in test mode, and a live key only in live mode.
