---
title: "Product release phases"
description: "What Private preview, Public preview and Generally available mean on an AgentaOS feature, and what to expect from each."
---

Every feature in the dashboard, the API and these docs is in one of three release phases. A page carries the phase in its sidebar tag and at the top of the page. No tag means the feature is generally available.

| Phase | Who can use it | How complete it is | Support |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Private preview** | By arrangement with us: tell us, and we support you directly. Some features need switching on for your account; the page says so. | Working end to end for the accounts that use it. Details can still change, and a change can break an integration. | Directly from us, at contact@agentaos.ai. |
| **Public preview** | Everyone with an AgentaOS account. Some features need us to switch them on for your account; the page says so. | Not feature-complete. New functionality can still arrive before release, and a change can break an integration. | contact@agentaos.ai and these docs. |
| **Generally available** | Everyone with an AgentaOS account. | Feature-complete. No breaking changes; any future change arrives through the SDK and CLI release notes. | contact@agentaos.ai and these docs. |

## In preview today

| Feature | Phase |
|---|---|
| [Platforms](/connect/overview): run your clients' businesses from one account and take a share of their sales. | Private preview |
| [Agent wallet](/experimental/agent-wallet): the crypto wallet an AI agent operates. | Private preview |
| [Stablecoin payouts](/payouts/stablecoin-payouts): be paid in USDC or EURC to a wallet instead of your bank account. | Public preview |

Before you use a feature in private preview, tell us at contact@agentaos.ai with the account it is for, so we can support you directly. If the feature's page says it needs switching on, we switch it on for that account and confirm by email.

## What a preview means for you

- The feature is on for your account, in test mode and in live mode, like any other feature.
- We tell you before a change that affects your integration, by email.
- The money rules do not change with the phase: AgentaOS is the merchant of record for every sale, and a preview feature's fees are on its own page.
