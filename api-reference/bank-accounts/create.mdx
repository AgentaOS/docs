---
title: "Add a bank account"
openapi: "POST /gateway/bank-accounts"
---

<Note>
Needs a live key. For a platform acting on a business it manages, ask us to open bank accounts by API for your platform first; until then the call gets `403 bank_accounts_by_api_closed`. The holder name is checked against the business's verified legal name at once: an account in another name is saved but never paid. Every account added through a platform's key is emailed to the platform's owner and to the business's owner. We keep the last four digits and a hash, never the number.
</Note>
