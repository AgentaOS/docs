---
title: "List businesses"
openapi: "GET /gateway/businesses"
---

<Info>
Every field on this resource is camelCase on the wire. See [Naming convention](/api-reference/introduction#naming-convention).
</Info>

<Note>
Returns a plain array, not the paginated envelope. These are the businesses **you** manage as a platform. To act as one of them on any other endpoint, send its `id` in the [`AgentaOS-Account` header](/api-reference/introduction#acting-for-a-business).
</Note>
