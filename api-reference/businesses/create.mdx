<Note>
The business starts in test mode and goes live after its own verification. With `clientEmail`, we email your client an invitation. The invitation expires after 7 days. With `sendInvitationEmail: false` we send nothing: send `inviteUrl` yourself, under your own brand.
</Note>

<Tip>
Store the returned `business.id` on your side, next to your record of the client. There is no metadata on a business: the id is the link between the two.
</Tip>
