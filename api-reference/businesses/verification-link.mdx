---
title: "Create a verification link"
openapi: "POST /gateway/businesses/{id}/verification-link"
---

<Warning>
Needs a live key (`sk_live_...`): the identity check is real. A test key gets `403 live_key_required`.
</Warning>

<Note>
You send the link; only your client can complete the identity check. `url` is `null` when they are already verified. Another app of your own company has no identity check of its own: `409 identity_from_company`.
</Note>
